The two mutations:

- **The protobuf read's diagnostics are dropped, and its records kept.** §7.2
  (`SPEC.md:2086–2088`) says a protobuf body's "`Records` are what the exchange

  library adds for an unknown or mistyped field is never seen. One hand-made
  body with an unknown field, asserting `exchange.records ==
  read_protobuf(body)`, would hold it.
- **The answer to a protobuf request is labelled `application/x-protobuf`**
  over the JSON `{}`. §7.2 (`SPEC.md:2182–2187`) says: "whichever media type
  it came in, with the response's `Content-Type` saying `application/json`
